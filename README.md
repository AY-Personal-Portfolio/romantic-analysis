# Quantitative Cross-Cultural Analysis of Romantic Preferences

Description: Utilized ML models to analyze individual's romantic preferences to predict infidelity

## Overview & Objective

Develop & implement two ML models (Logistic Regression & XGBoost) to predict whether an individual has cheated on their partner based on specific characteristics

## Methods
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Statistical Analysis
- ML
- Model Evaluation

## Languages & Tools
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Plotly
- Scikit-learn
- XGBoost
- Statsmodels
- SciPy
- SHAP

## Key Findings

1. The Logistic Regression model consumed 0.000036 kWh of energy and produced effectively 0.0 kg of CO emissions within a runtime of approximately 2.48 seconds with a Brier Score of 0.1886 and F1-Score of 0.6972.
2. The XGBoost model consumed 0.015509 kWh of energy while emitting 0.004151 kg of CO emissions with a runtime of 17.5 minutes and produced a Bier Score of 0.178 and F1-Score of 0.724.
3. Overall, the two models can moderately predict whether an individual has cheated based on certain characteristics, there is still much room for improvement.
